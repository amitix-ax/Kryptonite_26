Version History & Changelog

v1.2.0 - Hybrid AI & Dynamic Routing Pipeline
13 SEP 2026
  • Trained and deployed a PyTorch LSTM model for high-precision time-series iceberg drift forecasting.
  • Implemented A* (A-Star) Pathfinding algorithm with U-Net semantic segmentation for intelligent hazard avoidance.
  • Added "Google Maps-style" dynamic auto-redirecting for moving iceberg traffic.
  • Integrated Gemini API for autonomous tactical briefings and natural language risk assessment.
  • Added fluid, glassy UI/UX components with dynamic pulsing markers for vessels and stations.
  • Restricted global Leaflet map bounds to strictly anchor to the Earth's geometry, preventing infinite panning.
v1.1.0 - Institutional Redesign & Tactical Modes
12 SEP 2026
  • Completely overhauled UI/UX to match DARPA/NASA/NCPOR institutional design standards.
  • Replaced neon "cyber" aesthetic with a professional Navy, Blue, and White palette.
  • Removed OpenStreetMap and integrated Google Maps (Satellite, Terrain, Hybrid) APIs.
  • Introduced native Calendar Date Picker for querying 365 days of historical generated drift datasets.
  • Added manual coordinate entry capability to Voyage Planner.
  • Introduced integrated Open-Meteo Route Weather forecasting module with automated risk detection.
  • Added new "Low Bandwidth Mode" for tactical operations in areas with restricted satellite internet (Antarctica).
v1.0.0 - Initial Physics Engine Deployment
14 JAN 2026
  • Deployed core Runge-Kutta 4th Order (RK45) physics simulation engine.
  • Integrated Leaflet mapping interface with U-Net predictive uncertainty cones.
